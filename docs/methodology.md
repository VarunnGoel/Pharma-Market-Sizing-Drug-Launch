# Methodology

## 1. Data
I used public data where possible and treated remaining values as illustrative assumptions. Public facts come from Census, PubMed/AHS, and company disclosures.

## 2. Funnel
Adult population × broad migraine/severe-headache prevalence proxy × diagnosis rate × treatment-engagement assumption × preventive candidate rate × Illustrative Commercial Access Factor. Calculations carry full precision and are rounded only for display.

## 3. Evidence versus model application
A published number can be factual while applying it to this funnel can still be an analyst modeling choice. The AMPP 56.2% figure is a published diagnosis result among people with migraine; this project applies it to the broader prevalence proxy as a simplified diagnosis input. The AHS approximately 40% figure is published prevention context; this project applies it as a simplified sequential candidate rate.

## 4. Commercial access
Clinical eligibility and commercial reachability are separate. The 2024 AHS statement says CGRP-targeting therapies are a first-line option for migraine prevention and initiation should not require trial and failure of non-specific preventive medication approaches. The 40% Illustrative Commercial Access Factor is therefore a hypothetical commercial-reach assumption, not a clinical rule.

## 5. Pricing
WAC and GTN are inputs. Net price is derived as **WAC × (1 − GTN)**. Scenario net prices are derived from the base net price using Downside 0.8x, Base 1.0x, and Upside 1.2x price factors.

## 6. Market sizing
The model emphasizes patient counts. Dollar values are shown only as theoretical annual value at the modeled net price; they are not estimates of total US migraine spending.

## 7. Revenue
Modeled annual net sales = commercially addressable pool × scenario market share × scenario net price. Each modeled patient represents one patient-year of revenue.

## 8. Scenarios
The **Illustrative 5-Year Adoption Ramp** uses simple Downside, Base, and Upside scenario assumptions. The percentages are not estimated from historical launch data.

## 9. Sensitivity
Sensitivity tests market share, net price, and addressable patient pool. Results are revenue spreads across the selected ranges, not estimates of a formal responsiveness measure.

## 10. Model assumptions
The simple five-year model assumes a constant addressable pool, constant net price within each scenario, no mid-year launch effect, no within-year discontinuation, no price erosion, no population growth, no competitor price response, no generic entry, and no indication expansion.

## 11. Limitations
Public data are not proprietary claims data. Studies use different populations and years. The prevalence input is a historical broad migraine/severe-headache proxy and may overstate a product-specific migraine population. The model includes no physician interviews, proprietary payer research, clinical efficacy analysis, profit model, or patient-level persistence model.

## 12. Source-to-model lineage
Census -> adult population  
Burch -> broad prevalence proxy  
AMPP -> diagnosis evidence  
AHS -> preventive-treatment context and current CGRP positioning  
Hepp -> persistence/discontinuation/switching context only  
Analyst assumption -> treatment engagement, commercial access, market share, and scenario price factors  
Hypothetical client input -> WAC / product profile
