# Sources

- **US Census Bureau - SCPRC-EST2023-18+POP:** July 1, 2023 total resident population and resident population age 18+. The exact US age-18+ estimate is 262,083,034.
- **Burch R, Rizzoli P, Loder E. Headache. 2018. PMID 29527677:** 15.3% three-month self-reported migraine or severe-headache prevalence in US adults. Used as a broad historical proxy.
- **Diamond S et al. Headache. 2007. PMID 17371352:** AMPP treatment-pattern study; 56.2% of people with migraine had ever received a medical diagnosis. The model applies this evidence to the broader prevalence proxy as a simplified modeling choice.
- **Charles AC et al. Headache. 2024. DOI 10.1111/head.14692:** AHS position statement supporting CGRP-targeting therapies as a first-line option for migraine prevention and stating preventive therapy is indicated in approximately 40% of patients with migraine. The project applies the approximately 40% figure as a simplified funnel input.
- **Hepp Z et al. Cephalalgia. 2017. PMID 27837173:** persistence, discontinuation, switching, and re-initiation patterns for oral migraine preventive medicines. Used only for persistence/discontinuation/switching context.
- **Pfizer 2023 Form 10-K:** Nurtec ODT/Vydura worldwide 2023 revenue of $928M; product revenue includes acute and preventive uses.
- **AbbVie FY2023 results:** Qulipta $405M US + $3M international = $408M global; Botox Therapeutic $2.991B global therapeutic revenue across indications.
- **Amgen FY2023 results:** Aimovig $303M US + $20M ROW = $323M global.

The full URLs and source labels are maintained in `data/source_registry.csv`.
