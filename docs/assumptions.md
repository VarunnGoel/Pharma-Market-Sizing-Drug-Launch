# Assumptions

## Public Facts
- **262,083,034 US adults age 18+ (2023):** Census July 1 resident population.
- **15.3% self-reported migraine/severe-headache prevalence:** three-month survey measure; used as a broad historical proxy rather than a product-specific migraine prevalence estimate.
- **56.2% ever-diagnosed:** AMPP estimate among people with migraine. The model applies this published figure to the broader prevalence proxy as a simplified modeling choice.
- **Approximately 40% preventive-treatment context:** AHS states preventive therapy is indicated in approximately 40% of patients with migraine. The model applies this evidence as a simplified sequential funnel input.

## Analyst Assumptions
- **32% treatment engagement:** used to separate diagnosed patients from the subset assumed actively engaged in prescription treatment; not a direct sequential rate from one study.
- **Illustrative Commercial Access Factor = 40%:** estimates the portion of the modeled preventive-treatment pool assumed commercially reachable by a new branded product. It is not clinical eligibility or a universal payer rule.
- **12% Base Year 5 market share:** illustrative scenario assumption, not a competitor adoption benchmark.
- **Scenario price factors:** Downside 0.8x, Base 1.0x, Upside 1.2x applied to the derived base net price.
- **Sensitivity ranges:** market share 8%–16%, net price 80%–120% of base net price, and addressable patient pool 80%–120% of base.

## Hypothetical Client Inputs
- **Client:** Calystren Therapeutics.
- **Product:** Project CYR-217; hypothetical post-approval oral CGRP receptor antagonist.
- **Illustrative WAC:** $12,500 per patient-year.
- **Illustrative Target Product Profile:** once-daily oral administration. Any differentiation is a hypothetical commercial attribute, not a proven treatment advantage.

## Derived Pricing Output
**Net Price = WAC × (1 − GTN)**

Using the current inputs:
- WAC = $12,500
- GTN = 40%
- Net price = $7,500

Net price is a derived output, not an independent model assumption.

## Most Important Assumptions
1. **Addressable patient pool** - combines several evidence sources and simplifying gates.
2. **Market share** - directly scales modeled product patients and revenue.
3. **Net price** - directly scales revenue and depends on hypothetical WAC and GTN inputs.
